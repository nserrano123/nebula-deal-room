# Agent task 6 · Proposal versions (a new meeting updates a proposal already sent)

In B2B a proposal is rarely final. The customer meets again, a new person joins, they ask for growth options, an environment for testing, the implementation time. Each meeting should produce the next version without rewriting the proposal by hand and without changing a price by accident.

## Flow

1. **Import (once per deal).** The proposal the customer has today is often a PDF made elsewhere. The importer agent reads it into lines, discounts, terms and text. Postgres (`fn_manual_quote`) recomputes the totals; they must match the totals printed in the PDF, and every price read must appear in the PDF, before `fn_import_proposal` saves it as Sent on its original date.
2. **New meetings.** Each Google Meet transcript becomes a deal brief on the same opportunity (`fn_save_brief`), dated with the real meeting date.
3. **Draft.** The updater agent receives the previous version, the meetings since then (briefs and transcripts), the owner's price list (`price_item`), the product facts and the owner's instructions. It returns the new lines, terms, the change list (each with the customer's words) and the full new text.
4. **Checks in code, before the owner sees it.**
   - A price must equal the previous version's price for the same concept or a price in the price list; anything else becomes **pending**.
   - The payment schedule cannot change unless the owner says so.
   - Every `request_quote` must appear word for word in the transcripts.
   - Every number in the text must exist in the prices.
5. **Postgres.** `fn_create_version` stores the version, links the meetings it answers, logs the customer requests (origin R) and computes the price differences line by line (origin P). A pending price blocks approval.
6. **Owner.** Fills pending prices (`fn_set_version_pricing`, Postgres recomputes), edits the text, approves, and presses **OK, send it**. On sending, the previous version becomes *Superseded* and its link tells the customer which version replaced it.

## Prompts

The source of truth for the wording is `web/src/mastra/agents/proposal-versions.ts` (importer and updater).

## Data

| Object | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `proposal.parent_proposal_id`, `version` | The chain of versions |
| `proposal.source` | N generated by Nebula · I imported |
| `proposal.pricing_mode` | C price list (`fn_quote`) · M negotiated (`fn_manual_quote`) |
| `proposal_brief` | Meetings a version answers |
| `proposal_change` | What changed and why (origin P price / R customer request; kind A added, M changed, R removed, C clarified) |
| `price_item` | Priced extras the agent may quote; `confirmed = false` marks a proposed price |
